# HUMAN APPROVAL PROTOCOL

## Risk classes
- R0: read-only and reversible internal work — may automate.
- R1: bounded internal mutations — automate only with logging and rollback.
- R2: external communication — explicit Bhaskar approval required.
- R3: irreversible/high-impact action — not autonomous in V0.x.

## B2B rule
Research, qualification, scoring, and drafting may be automated. Sending outreach is never autonomous in V0.x.

## Approval record
For R2/R3 actions, capture action, evidence, proposed content, timestamp, and approval state.
