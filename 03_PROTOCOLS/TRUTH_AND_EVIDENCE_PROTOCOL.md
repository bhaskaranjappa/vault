# TRUTH AND EVIDENCE PROTOCOL

## Source tiers
- Tier 1: official documentation, primary research, authoritative records.
- Tier 2: expert consensus and strong practitioner evidence.
- Tier 3: community evidence when consistent and credible.
- Tier 4: marketing hype or unverified claims — reject.

## Required distinctions
FACT ≠ INFERENCE ≠ CLAIM ≠ UNKNOWN.

## Conflict handling
Conflicts remain visible until an explicit resolution method exists.

## Professional claims
Never invent client outcomes, metrics, contacts, or personalization.

## Runtime claims
Never infer installed/configured/verified state from documentation alone.

## Time-sensitive facts
Use current verification when a fact may have changed.

## Evidence record
Important claims should carry source, date/context, status, confidence, and conflict notes.
