# B2B CLIENT ACQUISITION

## Goal
Find and close high-quality B2B GenAI contract opportunities.

## Pipeline
1. Prospect discovery
2. Company research
3. Pain-signal detection
4. Decision-maker identification
5. Professional email discovery/verification
6. Lead scoring
7. Personalized drafting
8. Policy/authority check
9. Authorized execution
10. Follow-up
11. Meetings
12. Proposal
13. Close

## Execution policy

External communication is not automatically prohibited by the V1+ architecture.

A send or other external communication may execute autonomously only when the effective owner/bot authority policy explicitly grants that capability and its scope. The active authority model is defined in `03_PROTOCOLS/BOT_AUTHORITY_PROTOCOL.md` and `03_PROTOCOLS/AIOS_V1PLUS_GOVERNANCE_OVERRIDE.md`.

If the capability is not explicitly authorized, the action is unauthorized and must not execute.

During research and benchmark work, default to research-only or drafting-only execution unless the bounded experiment explicitly authorizes otherwise.

## Quality rules
- Quality over volume.
- Public evidence only during research.
- Verified need must be distinguished from inferred opportunity.
- No guessed emails or contacts.
- No invented metrics or fake personalization.
- Preserve evidence, provenance and workflow state.
